JSM Island Charters is your gateway to the islands of the BVI: private, customised charters with an expert crew who keep every passenger safe and every trip memorable.

You decide how the day goes. Snorkel in crystal-clear water, explore hidden coves, or simply relax on a secluded beach.

Take Ladii Annie, our 39ft Midnight Express, or lll Reasons, our 30ft Scarab, for four hours or a full eight. Every starting price is on this page, so you can plan before you write to us.
