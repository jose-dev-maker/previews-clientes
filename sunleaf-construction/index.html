How we build

  1. First you call

    Tell us what you want to build and where. Call or WhatsApp, 7am to 7pm.

  2. Then we visit the land

    We come to the plot, look at the slope, the access and the ground.

  3. Then the price

    A written quote with the work, the materials and how long it takes.

  4. Foundations

    Footings dug, steel tied and the base poured, before anything rises.

  5. Columns and slab

    Reinforced concrete columns and the floor slab that ties them together.

  6. Block walls

    Concrete block, course by course, with the openings for doors and windows.

  7. The roof

    A concrete roof slab, levelled by hand, or a roof tied down for hurricane season.

  8. Finishes, then the keys

    Plaster, paint, doors and windows. We walk it with you, then it is yours.

Our track record

After Hurricane Irma, the Caribbean Development Bank lent the BVI money to rebuild. One of the contracts, in 2019, was the concrete pads for the territory's weather station and seismic equipment. It went to Sunleaf.
