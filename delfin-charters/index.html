Good to know

Where do you pick us up?

At Señor Frog’s downtown, close to the cruise port, which suits cruise, Baha Mar and Nassau guests. Staying at Atlantis or on Paradise Island? We collect you at Jimmy Buffett’s Margaritaville.

What if my ship changes its schedule?

If the cruise line changes the itinerary, you can move your trip to another day or get a full refund. We get cruise guests back in time for their sailing.

What is included?

Snacks, water, soft drinks and alcohol on every trip, plus snorkel gear, life jackets and a safety briefing. The Exuma and Eleuthera charters include lunch. Food and drinks at the Rose Island beach bar are paid there.

What should I bring?

Swimwear, a towel, a hat and sunscreen, and a waterproof camera for the turtles and the pigs. Everything for the water is on board.

Can I cancel?

More than 72 hours before departure, we refund 50% of the price. Within 72 hours, or if you don’t show, there is no refund, and private charters are not refundable. If we cancel for weather, you get a full refund or a new date.

Is it right for everyone?

The boat is fast, so the trips aren’t suitable for children under two, during pregnancy, or with back or neck injuries, broken bones or heart conditions. Every guest signs a waiver before we leave.
